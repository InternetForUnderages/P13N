## About me
I'm actually 11 years old, and joined GitHub with parental consent. I maked this account at 9, and now. I'm making content for InternetForUnderages and developing programs and softwares.
## What is P13N
It's an story about me, From 8 years old to now. About the pain, Laws and what is wrong about **Internet Age Safety**
## Are you trying to bypass safety filters and get on adult places
Of course not! InternetForUnderages is trying to make a kinder space for young users. We are not here to say:
> Let me post on X

We want to say :
> Let me use Reddit safely. Don't just "erase" me

It is what we want to do!
