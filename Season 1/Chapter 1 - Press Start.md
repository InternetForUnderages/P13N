# S1 - Chapter 1 - Press Start
## The beginning
One day. I was playing on my mom's  phone just like always. I was 8, playing Ninja Arashi, And then. A question popped up in my head. How this games are made? So I searched for answer. From google results to endless questions from my mom. From Videos to Articles. And finally I met some little programs. Like "Platformer Builder", for a while, I was stuck on these, 
## Hello World? Hello Finica!
Hours of struggle and trying. Playing and joy. until... I met an app called Finica. It was a super simple blocky coding app. It had blocks like "Move", "Turn" or "Attack". They were not making a game. And I was a little mad but kept continuing.
## The Ad that changed my life forever
For 5 full days. Finica was my closest door to programming world.
Next day. When I opened the app. There was an ad. For an app called Finica (I know it's confusing). Not the same app. Another product of their team. "Finica Adult". I clicked on ad. Downloaded the app. And **BOOM**. See in next chapter!
### Chapter's lesson (Fun)
Never use an adblocker. Because an might change you path and existence.
